# GRTQC causal audit — 2 October 2026

The immediate requirement is reliable learned actor improvement below 24.263 seconds. The long-term requirement remains a confirmed Summer 1 GRTQC lap below 22.000 seconds. Neither requirement is met yet. The immutable TQC source is unchanged; its policy SHA-256 is `ffbea4ca57116cd2586c17ccec4fc761600e0d1ee5c6d94e31b98122220daece`.

## Reference mathematics

The [GRTQC publisher preview](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0957417426014302) describes gated actor/critic layers and a variance penalty across critic predictions. Full method equations were not accessible and no public author implementation was located. Exact author-specific gate placement and variance reduction cannot be independently claimed from that preview. PolyBot's choices remain explicit: actor `ReLU(h) * 2*sigmoid(Wg*h+bg)` with identity initialization, critic scale one, and population variance across critics at corresponding quantile indices averaged across states/quantiles. This is an implementation of the described design, not a verified reproduction of unavailable author code.

The base algorithm was checked against the [TQC authors' paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.04269), [SB3 Contrib implementation](https://github.com/Stable-Baselines-Team/stable-baselines3-contrib/blob/master/sb3_contrib/tqc/tqc.py), and installed package source. With extra penalty and step restriction disabled, a regression test compares actual actor, critics, target networks and entropy updates over identical replay samples and random draws. They agree numerically. Quantiles are pooled across critics, sorted, and the upper configured number per critic is dropped from that pooled mixture. Targets have no gradient and use `r + (1-done)*discount*(truncated_target_quantiles - alpha*log_pi)`. Actor loss uses the mean of all current quantiles, as TQC does. Automatic entropy gradients detach log probability; actor/critic rates are separate and apply after loading. The discrepancy under investigation is not established to be a reversed loss sign or truncation-order error.

## Recorded teacher-manifold evidence

`tools/audit_grtqc_pipeline.py` collected a fresh exact TQC lap using unchanged source control transforms, 30 ticks per decision and the current reward profile: 809 decisions, finish at 24.263 seconds. It records actual 109-input observations, rewards, transitions and executed tick controls in `logs/grtqc-source-manifold-109.npz` and its JSON telemetry sidecar.

The stopped 136,897-decision learner's audit on those states found mean Q 45.872 against mean discounted driving return 53.324, absolute error 7.543, mean absolute one-step TD error 0.444, and quantile disagreement 0.0408. The recorded-return comparison excludes entropy and truncation, so some pessimism is expected; it is not an exact soft-value residual. The two critics' action-gradient cosine averaged 0.552. Agreeing value estimates therefore did not imply agreeing local action slopes. Actor gradient norm was 7.364 with finite layer gradients. Actor gates were near 0.5 and none saturated; roughly 95% of first-layer critic gates and 54–63% of second-layer critic gates were saturated. These are measurements, not proof that saturation is the sole cause.

The first rejected continuation update changed deterministic controls by at most 0.000538 on these real teacher states. Its own critic predicted mean Q improvement of only 0.000111. Live evaluation crashed at 23.4% progress. Fractions one half and one quarter completed five laps at 24.619 and 24.739 seconds, both slower than the starting 24.485 cleaner candidate. Fractions one eighth through one sixty-fourth still failed, so smaller parameter distance is not monotonically safer. Predicted improvement is vastly smaller than measured value/TD errors. A positive average Q delta alone is insufficient acceptance evidence. Artifacts are `logs/grtqc-backtrack-first-live.json` and `logs/grtqc-backtrack-first-predicted-values.json`.

## Confirmed state and termination defects

The previous observation omitted elapsed time and checkpoint index although these determine milestone rewards. It also omitted peak/start progress, applied previous control duty and wrapper failure/grace timers, although these determine progress credit, failure clawback, action cost, contact filtering and termination. Physical inputs could therefore be identical while task rewards or termination differed. Regression tests demonstrate this aliasing. Optional `critic_environment_state` appends twelve actual normalized context values after the four PWM values. Critics receive all 121 inputs; the actor still receives its original 105 inputs plus four controller inputs, with the inherited matrix multiply preserved. The new schema is `polybot.observation.v2.training-state`; old 105/109-input transitions cannot be padded into learning replay. They remain archived for diagnosis.

An incomplete task deadline incurred failure/clawback penalties but was returned as a bootstrap-preserving truncation. A target then combined failure cost with continued value from the discarded attempt. Such deadlines are now absorbing failures, while artificial curriculum boundaries remain truncations that bootstrap from the real final observation. A replay regression verifies the different masks. Reward semantics are versioned `nonterminal-contact-v3-deadline`, so older replay must be recollected before learning under the new task semantics. The physics, PWM scheduler and source driving weights are unchanged.

## Replay audit and remaining work

The source archive has 25,384 old 105-input transitions, 28 positive terminal outcomes and two negative outcomes. The current GRTQC archive has 136,896 stored 109-input transitions, 104 positive terminal outcomes, 101 negative outcomes and 85 bootstrap-preserving resets/timeouts. Only 5.3% of current states exceed 90% progress, versus 7.8% in the source archive. Its early-state occupancy is much higher. Nineteen complete episodes still match the immutable actor; their mean driving return is 53.322. These counts alone do not justify dropping failures or indiscriminately importing old replay. Layout and reward differences prevent a valid direct merge.

Still to resolve and measure: complete action representation across overlays/touchdown release, optimizer/update telemetry, actor/critic gradient conditioning, replay weighting and teacher-state calibration, reward ranking of measured complete laps, and an actor acceptance strategy backed by real deterministic completion and speed. Ordinary continuation was stopped cleanly rather than left repeating destructive update/recovery cycles. Diagnostic backtracking is not an accepted faster policy.

## Measured reward contradiction and pace selection

Fresh complete lap captures under the old profile gave the 24.263-second, two-contact source scaled reward 93.7335 and the slower 24.485-second, one-contact candidate 97.7278. Their start-state discounted driving returns were 50.6455 and 52.0535 respectively. The old objective therefore preferred the slower lap. An extra contact cost 600 raw points while the 0.222-second pace difference changed finish credit by only 71.676 points. Recovery also deliberately preferred the cleaner candidate despite its slower time. This was a task-objective/selection conflict, not evidence that lower learning rate would help.

The causal profile keeps negative contact cost (-25), strengthens elapsed cost (-20/second), and removes reference-corridor speed, unsafe-speed, air-brake and ground-brake shaping after reliable completion. Finish and progress/failure credit remain. Reweighting the same recorded transitions gives total rewards 87.9486 versus 87.4341 and discounted start returns 47.3501 versus 46.9185, correctly preferring the faster measured lap. This establishes this pair's ranking, not a universal optimal policy. The captured aggregate terms are preserved as a regression fixture; full per-step evidence is in `logs/grtqc-reward-ranking.json` and the two manifold captures.

Optional pace-only acceptance promotes only a five-finish faster policy and uses initialization or the fastest champion for recovery. A slower clean lap does not change its actor reference or recovery source. Failed and slower candidates remain diagnosable and are rejected after the first check in the causal profile, rather than letting five destructive checks accumulate. This limits damage; it does not establish that proposals themselves have become good.

## Action labels, candidate experience and optimization

A regression demonstrates two different raw longitudinal demands collapsing to the same post-overlay air-brake action while retaining different controls to resume on touchdown. The two-number post-transform label therefore does not uniquely describe this environment action. The exact captured teacher lap had no states in the single-air-layer tail where this specific ambiguity is apparent, so this defect is not proven to cause its observed first-update failure.

Optional raw-policy replay stores the demand before fixed transforms, uses that same demand in critic targets and actor loss, and lets the unchanged output transforms/PWM/touchdown scheduler define the environment mapping. Noise is applied before those transforms. Deterministic evaluation remains unchanged. Raw and post-transform replay cannot be mixed; initialization metadata declares `grtqc.raw-policy.v1`. Fresh physical/controller/task observations are required. Entropy now describes the same raw random variable supplied to the critic rather than a noninvertibly transformed label.

The causal profile computes actor gradients on states from the latest verified lap, without prescribing actions or adding action imitation. Critics continue sampling all replay, including failures. This addresses the measured mismatch between early/failure-heavy replay occupancy and where a proven driver needs local improvement. It is state selection, not prioritized critic sampling, and its effect still needs live measurement.

Previously, actor evaluation failures were discarded from replay. Optional evaluation retention now records actual raw demands, rewards and real terminal observations from complete screen/confirmation attempts. Unfinished collection is closed as a bootstrap-preserving artificial boundary before append; no reset state is spliced into a physical transition. Incomplete transport-failed evaluation samples are discarded on retry. This supplies the critic with the exact counterfactual behavior used for acceptance, while keeping ordinary off-policy replay targets. Evaluation decisions are additional experience, separately logged rather than added to the requested training budget.

Actor and critic total/per-layer gradient and executed parameter-update norms, Adam step count, and existing Q/action-drift metrics now survive logger dumps and checkpoint round trips. Restore deliberately clears actor and entropy optimizer moments, retaining critic optimizer and replay. A regression starts with nonempty actor Adam state and verifies recovery clears it. Finite gradient checks fail explicitly. Current base TQC numerical parity remains tested. Parameter interpolation still retains the computed Adam state intentionally; no evidence establishes stale moments as the original failure, and rejected actor recovery discards them.

Replay seeding now checks raw/post-transform action semantics, marks an unfinished buffer join as a truncation instead of implying physical continuity, and invalidates policy-specific value-reference caches/initialization counters. Old TQC replay remains incompatible with this new experiment and is not imported or padded. Existing PWM/landing physics and all source overlays remain untouched.

Evaluation no longer labels a nonterminal barrier contact as a crash. Target completion requires at least five finishes with median below the requested threshold, rather than accepting a single fast outlier. Resume retains an archive speed schedule when legacy metadata is empty, matching standalone initialization/evaluation behavior.

## Experiment status and limits

`profiles/training/summer-1-grtqc-causal-30.json` uses the exact immutable TQC graft, 109 actor inputs and 121 real critic inputs, raw-policy action labels and newly collected replay. Five paired live initialization laps reproduced 24.263 seconds with zero action/time drift (`logs/grtqc-causal-initialization-live.json`). This proves transfer/control fidelity only. The old 136,897-decision precise-values run remains stopped and preserved.

The initial context/deadline repair passed the complete 272-test suite and Ruff and was pushed as 8361690. Further runtime/action/selection checks are recorded with subsequent commits. The causal experiment is a justified repair bundle, not a one-factor ablation proving which defect dominates. Ordinary actor gradients may still be unreliable, particularly given PWM feedback sensitivity and saturated critic gates. Actual learned five-finish pace checks, not calibration losses or identical frozen laps, determine whether the new loop is healthier. The sub-24.263 and sub-22 requirements remain unmet until measured.
